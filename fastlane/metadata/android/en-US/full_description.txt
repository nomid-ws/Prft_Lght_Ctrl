Prft Light Ctrl is a privacy-first, 100% offline Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) studio lighting controller built in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for modern Android (Android 12–16+) phones and tablets — no account wall, no internet permission, and no location tracking.

Why Prft Light Ctrl:
• Zero Account or Cloud Login: Opens straight to your lights — never get locked out in a basement studio or field shoot with no signal.
• 100% Offline & Private: Requests zero INTERNET permission and uses Android 12+ neverForLocation BLE scanning (never asks for GPS/Location).
• Reliable Android BLE Reconnects: Pinned-kit auto-reconnect, foreground keepalive when locking your phone, and hardware-filtered 1M scanning across modern Android phones.
• Fast Studio Workflow: Saved Studio Scenes with offline JSON share, Named Group Roles (Key, Fill, Rim), Dual Power Readout (Watt-seconds & 1/1..1/512 fractions alongside 1–10 scale), Portrait + Landscape 6-fader console, thumb-zone FIRE button, and optional Volume Key FIRE trigger.

Hardware Compatibility:
• Physically Verified (Direct BLE): Profoto B10X Plus/Pro, B10X, B10 Plus & B10 (flash + bi-color 3000–6500 K LED), Profoto A2 (100 Ws flash + modeling LED), Profoto Connect Pro (100-ch AirX 6-group A–F remote, Auto TTL/Manual, Per-Group TTL ratios, Sync/HSS/Global Shutter, AF-Assist, RF Power), and Profoto Connect.
• Protocol-Compatible Direct BLE (Catalog-Mapped): AirX strobes/packs sharing core PUP opcodes (A10, B20/B30, D30, Pro-D3, Pro-B3, Pro-11, C1/C1 Plus).
• Indirect Control via Connect Pro / Connect: Pre-Bluetooth Air strobes (B1, B1X, B2, D1, non-AirX D2) lack built-in BLE — control their groups over 2.4 GHz Air via a BLE-linked Connect Pro or Connect remote.
• Not Supported: Cinema RGBWW/HSI/Gel/SFX/DMX fixtures (L600C/D/X, L1600D, ProPanel 3x2) and non-Bluetooth Air Remote TTL triggers.

Known Limitations:
• Lighting controller only — does not capture smartphone camera photos or sync phone shutter to flash.
• No firmware updates: Offline by design (0 INTERNET permission) and DFU/bootloader GATT characteristics are isolated to prevent mid-shoot bricking. Use Profoto's desktop USB updater (B10/B10X, A2, Connect) or the official Profoto Control app (portless Connect Pro) when updating firmware.
• One active BLE app connection per fixture — close the official Profoto app before scanning.
• Android 11 and older are not supported (supports modern Android 12–16+).
