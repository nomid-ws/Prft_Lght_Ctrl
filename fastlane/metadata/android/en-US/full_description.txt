Prft Light Ctrl is an open-source, offline Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) studio lighting controller engineered in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android 12+ phones and foldables (Portrait, Landscape, and foldable displays).

Supported Hardware:
• Profoto B10X Pro / Plus & B10X (500 Ws / 250 Ws flash energy + 3250 lm bi-color 3000–6500 K continuous modeling light)
• Profoto A2 (100 Ws flash energy + LED modeling light)
• Profoto Connect Pro (100-channel Air 2 / AirX 6-group A–F studio remote, including LE Coded PHY Air Range mode)
• Profoto Connect (buttonless AirTTL / Manual / Off remote, studio group standby, Air modeling toggle, and master TTL EV trim)

Key Features:
• 4-Tab Studio Console (Groups / Lights / LED / Devices): Non-scrolling 6-channel vertical studio faders (Groups A–F, 1.0–10.0 f-stops), ±0.1-stop steppers, ON/OFF group mute toggles, master ALL studio ratio trims, group-filtered fixture cards, and dedicated continuous/modeling LED console.
• Custom Equipment Nicknames: Label units by studio role (e.g., Key Light, Hair Light, Main Trigger) while keeping the hardware model permanently visible.
• Monochrome Black & White Studio UI: Matte charcoal dark palette (#0D0D0F–#222226) with high-contrast #FFFFFF studio controls designed for dark studio sets without pure-black OLED halation.
• Dual-UUID + Dual-PHY Native BLE Engine: Simultaneous LE 1M and LE Coded PHY scanning of SIG 0xFDBB and legacy 128-bit GATT services, MTU 255 negotiation, session authentication, and priority out-of-band Test Flash triggering.
• Bootloader / DFU Quarantine: Strict compile-time and runtime blocklists prevent any access to firmware-update GATT UUIDs or DFU opcodes.
• 100% Offline & Tracker-Free: Zero INTERNET permission, zero location tracking (neverForLocation BLE scan), zero analytics, and zero proprietary SDKs.
